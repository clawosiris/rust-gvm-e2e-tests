## Deployment-Aware Coverage Roadmap
- New issue [#127](https://github.com/clawosiris/rust-gvm-e2e-tests/issues/127) proposes replacing the exact Community-only feature baseline with deployment contracts, evidence-backed capability discovery, and deterministic test planning shared across Community and Enterprise deployments.
- The proposed policy makes missing or unhealthy required components fail discovery instead of silently reducing coverage, while optional unavailable capabilities remain explicitly not selected. Decisions are still needed on Enterprise provisioning ownership, mandatory feature flags, deterministic fixtures, and support for older `get_features` response shapes.
